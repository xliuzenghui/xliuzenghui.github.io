abstract: >-
 This study develops a CNN-LSTM framework that integrates fire-source spatial information with sensor time-series data to predict key fire hazards, including CO, O₂, and CO₂. By training 288 models with different sampling intervals and time windows, we show that prediction accuracy is mainly governed by the number of effective data points rather than simply by high-frequency sampling. The results indicate that about 20 effective data points are sufficient for stable model performance, providing a practical basis for balancing prediction accuracy, sensor cost, and computational demand.
